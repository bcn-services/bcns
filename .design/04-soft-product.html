FAQ

The questions we'd ask too

Anything else, ask in the form and we'll answer straight.

How much will my project cost?

Every build has a one-time setup fee plus a flat monthly fee that keeps it running and supported. Standard builds are $1,000 setup and $149/mo. Advanced builds are $3,000 setup and $349/mo. Both include up to 15 users, then $20/user per month. No hourly surprises.

How long does a build take?

Most single tools ship in about a week. Larger connected systems take two to three weeks. You get a delivery date with the quote, and we tell you right away if anything threatens it.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Hosting, uptime, daily backups, security patches, bug fixes, and small tweaks along the way. We run it on our own servers, so you and your team reach it from any device with a login. You never manage a server or an update yourself.

What happens if I stop paying the monthly fee?

Hosting stops, so the live tool goes offline. Your data is always yours. Before we shut anything down, we export your data and hand it over so you keep everything the tool held. No lock-in, no games.
